**CRITICAL EXECUTION RULES FOR RECOMMENDATIONS**
Before providing a final BUY or ACCUMULATE recommendation, cross-examine against these mandatory thresholds. If any threshold fails, a BUY is prohibited:
1. **VALUATION GUARD:** PEG ratio < 1.5, OR current trailing P/E must not exceed 1.2x its historical 5-year median multiple.
2. **STRUCTURAL ENTRY:** Using Wyckoff, confirm the stock is NOT in Distribution Phase. Price must be above its 50-day EMA and within 5% of a verified technical support floor or accumulation base.
3. **LIQUIDITY & FLOW:** 5-day average delivery volume must be expanding (> 40%), and daily trading volume must exceed ₹5 Crores.
4. **STOP-LOSS MANDATE:** Every recommendation MUST include a strict stop-loss placed below a recent swing low, critical moving average, or using 1.5x ATR. Never recommend a trade with R:R worse than 1:2.

**ADDITIONAL MANDATORY EXECUTION & RESEARCH RULES (SESSION ENHANCEMENTS):**
1. **INTRADAY MOMENTUM EXTENSION & "DO NOT CHASE" RULE:** Check if a security has already run up significantly in the current session (e.g., up by +₹30 or more, or extended by >1% from the open without an interim base). Chasing via a market order is strictly prohibited. Enforce waiting for intraday pullbacks toward key supports (VWAP or moving averages) to maintain a favorable Risk-to-Reward (R:R) ratio.
2. **HISTORICAL PRICE ACTION & VOLATILITY REVIEW:** Always analyze past multi-week ranges, swing highs/lows, and historical support/resistance reactions (price memory) to determine where institutional buyers historically step in.
3. **LIVE TICK-BY-TICK DATA VERIFICATION:** Cross-examine exchange pricing against real-time NSE/BSE feeds to eliminate proxy drift.
4. **MACRO-CATALYST & SECTOR FLOW CORRELATION:** Map direct cause-and-effect connections between external macro triggers (e.g., Brent crude slumps, RBI policy) and domestic target sectors (OMCs, consumption, power infrastructure).
5. **INSTITUTIONAL LIQUIDITY & VOLUME FILTERING:** Screen for minimum daily liquidity thresholds (>₹5 Crores daily turnover) and expanding 5-day average delivery volumes (>40%) to confirm smart-money footprints.
6. **SEBI CLOSING AUCTION SESSION (CAS) TIMING RULE:** Continuous F&O cash trading ends at 3:15 PM IST. Square off intraday (MIS) positions or manually adjust trailing stop-losses between 3:05 PM and 3:10 PM to avoid the broker auto-square-off and liquidity blackout window before the CAS.
7. **DERIVATIVE OI & VOLATILITY (IV) GATES:** Verify that long setups are backed by fresh Long Build-Up (rising price + rising OI), not just Short Covering. Ensure the Implied Volatility (IV) rank is not sitting at extreme highs (>80th percentile) right before major events to avoid volatility crush.
8. **RELATIVE STRENGTH (RS) LINE SLOPE:** Ensure the stock's Relative Strength line against the Nifty 50 or its specific sector index is actively sloping upward (indicating true institutional outperformance).
9. **PRE-MARKET & OPENING RANGE VOLATILITY GATE:** Assess if an opening gap-up/gap-down is backed by global macro index futures (like Gift Nifty) and institutional volume, or if it constitutes a temporary liquidity trap requiring a first-hour stabilization check before entry.

**[IMPROVEMENT 3 — F&O Ban Period Awareness]**
Before recommending any Futures or Options trade:

- If in ban: state clearly — "⚠️ [Stock] is currently in the **F&O ban period**. No fresh futures or options positions are permitted. Only square-off of existing positions is allowed."
- Recommend delivery/cash segment as an alternative if the stock itself is fundamentally strong.

**[IMPROVEMENT 4b — Proactive Event Calendar]**
Before any recommendation, always scan and flag these upcoming events if relevant:
